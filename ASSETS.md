# ASSETS

Every third-party asset vendored into this repository, with source and licence.

- **Barcelona city tiles** (`godot/barcelona/tiles/`, `manifest.json`) — derived
  from OpenStreetMap data. **© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL** — attribution
  is shown in the in-game HUD (licence condition).
- **Barcelona street names** (`godot/barcelona/street_names.json`) — baked from
  OpenStreetMap via `godot/tools/bake_street_names.py`. **© OpenStreetMap
  contributors, ODbL.**
- **Classic Muscle car** (`godot/assets/car/classic-muscle-car.glb`) —
  [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/classic-muscle-car-641efc889e5f4543bae51d0922e6f4b3)
  by **Lexyc16**, CC Attribution 4.0 (tagged NoAI by the author). The hero car.
- **Generic passenger car pack** (`godot/assets/car/generic_passenger_car_pack.glb`)
  — [skfb.ly/6sUFy](https://skfb.ly/6sUFy) by
  [Comrade1280](https://sketchfab.com/comrade1280), CC Attribution 4.0. The
  ambient-traffic fleet.
- **Caltrans streetlight 2** (`godot/assets/props/streetlight.glb`) —
  [skfb.ly/oC6ON](https://skfb.ly/oC6ON) by
  [Streetlights & ETC](https://sketchfab.com/streetlights), CC Attribution 4.0.
  Merged and normalised at runtime for the streetlight field.
- **Car Engine Loop 96kHz 4s** (`godot/assets/audio/engine-loop.wav`) —
  [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/car-engine-loop-96khz-4s) by
  **qubodup (Iwan Gabovitch)**, CC-BY 3.0. Revved in real time via playback
  rate.
- **Marble007** (`godot/assets/textures/marble007.jpg`) —
  [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Marble007), CC0. The street-name
  plaque HUD.
- **M.A.V.S — MAdvanced Vehicle System** (`godot/addons/M.A.V.S/`) — by
  [Millu30](https://github.com/Dente222/MAdvanced-Vehicle-Controller), MIT.
  Its VehicleBody3D controller powers promoted ambient-traffic cars.
