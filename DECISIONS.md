# DECISIONS

Deviations from the brief and notable technical choices, one line each.

- **Dev port 5174** — 5173 was occupied by an unrelated dev server on this machine.
- **M1 placeholder wheels are smooth cylinders** — rolling is not visually verifiable until real rims land in M4; spin math is in place.
- **Axis-aligned street grid instead of free splines** — exact analytic road-space mapping per-pixel in the shader (wear, lanes, gutters are procedural and resolution-independent); visual richness comes from profile/wear/materials, not plan curvature.
- **Road material = full custom WGSL ShaderMaterial, not a PBR plugin** — Babylon 8.56's WGSL PBR lacks a pre-lighting roughness hook, which blocks the wetness→roughness collapse; a custom shader also owns the layered water BRDF, anisotropic streak speculars, retroreflection and glints outright.
- **Planar reflection (MirrorTexture) instead of SSR for the road** — the street is locally planar; a mirrored render captures offscreen neon above the frame, structurally eliminating the "reflection ends at top of screen" defect. SSR dropped entirely.
- **Car-following single ortho shadow map (4096, PCF) instead of CSM** — the world is four blocks; one tight follow frustum gives crisper texels than 3 cascades, one code path for both the custom road shader (manual PCF) and stock materials.
- **Street lighting = custom light array in the road shader (diffuse pools + anisotropic wet streaks) + emissive geometry** — no Babylon per-mesh light lists needed; the road is where light response matters.
- **Hero car is built procedurally (lofted cross-sections)** — exhaustive search found no CC0 car meeting the quality bar (Poly Haven has none; Khronos CarConcept is CC-BY + logos + supercar); a parametric coupe we control beats a compromised asset.
- **Quality presets low/medium/high, default medium** — per direction to lower hardware demands; high restores the original RTX-class targets. Preset switch reloads (pipelines are built per preset).
- **HDR shader outputs clamped ≤120** — anything above half-float max poisons the bloom chain with NaN tiles (this bug shipped as a "black box in the sky" for an afternoon).
- **Motion blur is screen-space (camera) not per-object** — in a chase-cam driving demo the camera IS the motion; per-object adds a geometry velocity buffer for marginal gain. Revisit only if wheel blur is missed.
- **Weather wetness/puddles integrate with first-order lag, never blended** — rain fills fast (τ≈7s), drying is slow (τ≈45–70s×evaporation), so the street stays soaked after rain stops and dries in real time.
- **No SSAO** — night scene is emissive-dominated; contact grounding comes from the AO blob, baked facade grime and canyon shadows. SSAO's cost on the lowered hardware target buys almost nothing here.
- **FXAA + post-sharpen instead of TAA** — TAA on a fast chase camera without full motion vectors ghosts the one thing that must stay crisp (the car); glints are hash-stable in world space so the main TAA benefit is moot.
- **No DoF** — "very restrained" at driving FOV/speeds rounds to off; dropped for clarity and frame budget.
- **Glide alt input: Shift** — RMB stays primary; Shift serves trackpads (and synthetic-event test rigs which cannot deliver right-button events).
- **Displaced-water ridge visuals removed** (user direction) — the bright wavy ridge ribbons along tyre tracks read as melting asphalt; tracks now show as cleared water + damp + rubber only. The state-buffer G channel and splat slot remain for possible re-tuning.
- **Procedural engine audio added** (user direction; supersedes the brief's "no audio") — pure WebAudio (3 oscillators + filtered noise), RPM from speed through fake gear ratios, louder under throttle/drift, idle at rest; starts on first input per autoplay policy; volume slider in the overlay. Later replaced by a recorded CC-BY loop revved via playbackRate (see ASSETS.md).
- **On-screen UI added** (user direction; supersedes the brief's "no HUD") — persistent bottom-centre controls hint and a bottom-right km/h speedometer, both in the loading screen's dim mono style, pointer-events none, 10 Hz DOM updates.
- **Endless procedural city** (user direction; supersedes the brief's four-block loop) — the plan became an infinite periodic grid (200×160 m cells); roads, curbs, buildings, props, signage and lights are streamed around the car under one shared ~3 ms/frame build budget and disposed behind it. All placement is seeded from cell coordinates, so revisited streets are always identical. The dead-end caps, Jersey-barrier closures and striped boards are gone with the city edge they closed off.
- **Motorway rows** — every 4th east-west road (row index ≡ 2 mod 4) is a 24.9 m dual carriageway: three 3.5 m lanes per side, dashed dividers + solid edge lines, per-carriageway crossfall, and a raised concrete median island carrying thin-instanced Jersey barriers (with gaps at the signalised junctions; the island itself flattens through junctions so crossing traffic never mounts a hump).
- **Skyline rings pushed to 450–1200 m and re-centred on the car in 80 m snaps** — the streamed real city now reaches ~430 m, so the impostor rings live beyond it and follow the drive; parallax still happens within each snap cell.
- **Colliders reworked** (user direction) — curbs are 13 cm of real heightfield, drivable at a speed-scrub + camera-kick cost; hard containment only where the world is solid: the building line (SDF-gradient pushback ~1 m off facades) and the motorway median (analytic wall, open at junctions to match the barrier gaps).
- **Three time-of-day states replace the five mood states** (user direction) — Day / Afternoon / Night on keys 1–3, ~4 s transitions (down from 11 s); rain and fogbank presets retired, night carries the wet-street hero look via the physical wetness lag.
- **Night sidewalk dimming** (user direction) — the pale paving/concrete PBR band receives no streetlight point lights, so its albedo now tracks ambient intensity (quadratic); sidewalks sink into the dark instead of glowing.
- **Hero car is now the vendored CC-BY "Classic Muscle car" by Lexyc16** (user direction; supersedes the procedural coupe, which remains as fallback) — normalised to the physics frame at load (orientation by span analysis, length 4.9 m, floor at y=0); the baked-in-place wheel groups are carved into steer/spin pivots INSIDE the glTF's own node space (re-parenting across the RH→LH root mangles mirrored transforms) with car-frame axle/up axes mapped into that space once.
- **glTF import is deferred into the live render loop (frame 14) and the loader's materials are replaced wholesale** — importing the GLB during the synchronous boot renders corrupts unrelated WebGPU PBR pipelines (sidewalks went black past ~15 m; other orderings blew materials out white), and the loader's material instances stayed poisonous even later. Deferred import + fresh plain PBRMaterials with the same colours is stable across every state and drive test.
- **Body attitude clamped to ±0.12 rad; terrain attitude split from acceleration lean** (user direction on curb behaviour) — pitch/roll from wheel heights now tracks steps near-instantly (DAMP 16) so the body climbs curbs instead of clipping through them, the soft spring keeps only the cinematic accel/brake lean, and kerb-strike spikes can no longer fold the car over.
- **Top speed raised to 55 m/s (~198 km/h)** (user direction) — accel 11 m/s²; the engine-audio gear table gains a 6th gear and the speedometer dial already read to 200.
- **Curved streets via domain warp** (user direction: "just a grid, no curves") — the whole plan is now evaluated in a smoothly warped coordinate frame (two-octave sine warp, ±6.5 m over ~200–430 m wavelengths, identical constants in cityPlan.js and the road shader), so every street and the motorway sweep in gentle S-curves while the analytic grid math stays exact and physics/visuals agree by construction. Followers: road chunks build masked grids trimmed to the true curved contour (bounds track the local warp range), the curb/sidewalk loop is generated in grid space then Newton-projected onto the warped SDF, buildings rotate rigidly to their street's local tangent (bow error < 15 cm over a frontage), and every prop/vfx placement maps through gridToWorld + tangent yaw. Streets bend, junctions stay coherent, revisits stay identical. Cost: masked street builds raise the worst incremental build slice to ~5 ms (still inside the shared budget).
- **Curb + sidewalk band promoted to a road-grade WGSL shader** (user direction: "align the quality gap between asphalt and the rest") — the ground band now shares the road's vocabulary: two-scale texture mixing with macro tone variation, the same street-light storage buffer (sodium pools + wet blinn sheen), the shadowed follow-map sun, live wetness darkening and the identical height-fog model. Replaces stock PBR for those meshes (manholes/drains stay PBR) and retires the night albedo-dimming hack, since the band is now actually lit by the same lights as the asphalt.
- **Driving physics pass** (user direction) — grip is now surface-aware: wet streets cut lateral grip (tunable `carWetGrip`, default 28% at full wet), stretch braking ~16%, soften launches, and let handbrake slides carry further; sidewalks grip ~28% less with double rolling drag. Launch traction: scrubbing tyres can't push at full force. A lateral-acceleration cap on the yaw target acts as ESC — full lock at 140+ km/h arcs wide instead of snap-spinning (measured 87°→1° peak slip), while gliding raises the cap so committed drifts are untouched (104° turn in 1.5 s at 70 km/h). Slip recovery is always-on now (was drift-only), steering response slows with speed, and reverse tops out at ~40 km/h.
